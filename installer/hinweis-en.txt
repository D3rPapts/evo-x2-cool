EVO-X2-COOL - learning fan and clock controller for the GMKtec EVO-X2

Please read before installing:

- Unofficial community tool. Not made, endorsed or supported by GMKtec, Sixunited or AMD.
- The program writes to the embedded controller (fan curve, power mode) and limits the CPU clock through the Windows power plan. Use it at your own risk.
- It only controls with EC firmware 1.10. With any other version it only measures and changes nothing.
- It needs the PawnIO driver (by namazso). If it is missing, this setup installs it.
- On exit and uninstall the BIOS fan curve comes back. The emergency mode (fan to full when overheating) cannot be switched off.
- Written with AI assistance (Claude Code), tested on real hardware.
